## ✨ What makes RazorAI different?

Traditional e-commerce makes the **user** navigate the store.

RazorAI lets an **AI agent** navigate the commerce experience.

```text
User
  ↓
Conversational AI
  ↓
Product Discovery
  ↓
Recommendations
  ↓
Cart Management
  ↓
Transaction Guardrails
  ↓
Razorpay
```

And when a transaction crosses the autonomous limit, RazorAI doesn't blindly proceed.

**AI autonomy stops. Human approval takes over.**

---

## 🛡️ AI that can shop — without unrestricted access to money

RazorAI is built around one principle:

> **The AI can request a transaction. The backend decides whether it is allowed.**

This enables autonomous commerce while maintaining:

**₹2,000 autonomous transaction limit**
**Human approval for higher-value purchases**
**Idempotency protection against duplicate transactions**
**Backend product & stock validation**
**Audit logging for agent actions**
